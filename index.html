01 / The problem

First, find
the source.

Hostile jammers disrupt drone operations. They must be located before they can be destroyed. Teams need a practical, drone-mounted way to find these sources and support their neutralization.

The product

Passive direction finding.
Built for integration.

The planned offering combines a sensor, onboard processing, calibration, mounting and integration support—so drone manufacturers do not have to build a specialist sensing system from scratch.
